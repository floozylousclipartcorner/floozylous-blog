I'm Having a Meltdown — Funny Tips for Surviving the Holiday Chaos

The lights are tangled, the cookies are... ambitious, and somebody just asked what's for dinner on December 23rd. If you're melting down over the holidays, take a number — our snowman lost it weeks ago and honestly? He's thriving. Here are his best survival tips:

1. Lower the bar, proudly. Store-bought cookies arranged on a nice plate are now an "artisan assortment." Nobody has ever once checked. Nobody ever will.

2. Take the Snowman Reset. Feel the melt starting? Make a cocoa (eggnog counts — no judgement here), step outside into the cold for two minutes, and come back roughly 40% less melty. Snowmen have known this trick forever.

3. Delegate exactly one thing. Not everything — one thing. The gravy. The playlist. The elf. Get it off your plate, literally, and watch your blood pressure send you a thank-you card.

4. Kitchen dance break. Music up, wooden spoon microphone, stir like nobody's watching. Scientifically proven* to help. (*Not science. Pure vibes. Still works.)

5. Remember January exists. The tree comes down, the sales start, and all of this becomes the story you tell next year. The meltdown is temporary. The photos are forever.

Market sellers, he does not sit on a table for long.

Here's to melting gracefully. Preferably near snacks. 🧀
