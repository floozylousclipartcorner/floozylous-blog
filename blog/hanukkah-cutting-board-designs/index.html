Hanukkah Cutting Board Designs for Your Holiday Table

Some holidays get a candle and a quick dinner. Hanukkah gets eight nights — and a table that deserves to look the part.

These are for the makers celebrating at home, and for anyone pressing a gift for Jewish friends or family who'll actually use it. Two designs, sold separately, so you can pick your favourite or press both and make a matching set.

They're made for glass cutting boards — the kind that sit out on the counter all eight nights, get used for real, and still look like they belong at the centre of the table. Press one as a hostess gift, or keep a set for your own holiday kitchen.

Hanukkah starts the evening of Friday, December 4th this year, so there's still time to press, wrap, and gift before the first candle.
