With God, All Things Are Possible — Even in October

October in my kitchen is a lot. There are pumpkins on every surface, something is always in the oven, somebody's costume needs hot glue at 9pm, and the cat has decided the counter is hers now. If your kitchen runs the same way this month, this one's for you.

Who is it for? The "spooky, but make it faith" crowd — and if that's you, you already know you're outnumbered at the Halloween party. It's for church friends who love fall hardest, hostesses who want their kitchen to say something true and seasonal, and anyone whose autumn decorating style is equal parts harvest festival and hallelujah.

What can you make with it? It's a sublimation PNG made for standard glass cutting boards — it presses up beautifully and makes a gift that lands somewhere between "happy Halloween" and "thinking of you," which is a very fun place for a gift to land. Craft fair tables, church raffles, pastor appreciation, your own counter leaning against the backsplash… all excellent life choices. It's a high-resolution 300 DPI instant download with commercial use included, so sellers are covered too.

Because here's the thing the design keeps reminding me, usually while I'm elbow-deep in pumpkin guts: all things really are possible. Even a calm October. (Results may vary. The cat helps.)
