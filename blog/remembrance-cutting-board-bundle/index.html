Made to Remember — A Remembrance Cutting Board Bundle

Some designs aren't about decorating a kitchen. They're about remembering.

This bundle is for the makers who mark November 11th properly — Canadian military families, veterans, and anyone who wants a piece in their home that means something. Three designs in one bundle, made to be pressed, gifted, and kept.

A cutting board isn't the usual way to remember, and that's rather the point. It sits in the middle of everyday life — morning coffee, Sunday dinner — and quietly says: we remember the ones who served, and the families who waited, too.

Press one for a veteran you love, one for a family who understands, or keep the set together as a keepsake for your own home. In time for Remembrance Day, November 11th.
