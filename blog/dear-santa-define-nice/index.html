Dear Santa, Define Nice — Cookie Exchange Tips for the Holidays

Cookie exchanges are the best part of December — and the easiest to overthink. After years of watching what actually gets eaten (and what quietly goes home again), here are the tips that matter.

Bake what travels well. Drop cookies, shortbread, and anything sturdy beats a frosted showpiece that smudges in the tin. If it can't survive a car ride, it can't survive an exchange.

Label everything. Your cookies' names and ingredients on a little card — allergies are real, and the card is what makes people reach for seconds instead of asking questions.

Bring a tin, not a bag. People take more when the container is pretty, and a tin full of cookies is a gift in itself. And bring something for the hostess, not just the exchange — she did the dishes.

Press a board for your own cookie station while you're at it — somewhere to pile the tin, the cocoa, and the evidence.

Dear Santa, define nice. I'll wait — cookie in hand.
