Pressing for Christmas Markets

Here's the honest truth about Christmas markets: shoppers aren't browsing, they're hunting. They want small, gift-priced things they can carry home in one hand — coasters, ornaments, and one good showpiece board for the cook on their list.

If you've got a December stall on the calendar, November is your make-or-break month. It's for pressing your proven sellers in batches. The last two weeks before a market are for restocking what sold, not for testing a blank you've never pressed before. If a blank already sold for you, that's your December lineup — don't gamble the season on an untested one.

Press in batches, keep your best sellers within reach, and leave yourself one evening to breathe before market day. Your future self, the one standing behind the table, will be grateful.
