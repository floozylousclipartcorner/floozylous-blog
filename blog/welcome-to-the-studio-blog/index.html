Welcome to the Studio Blog

Hi, and welcome in! This little corner of the internet is where both Floozy Lou's Etsy shops — FloozyLousClipart and FloozyLousDesigns — get to stretch out beyond the shop shelves, together in one place. Clipart sets live alongside Frame TV art, printable cards, templates, and milestone boards.

Here's what you'll find here:

New drops. Every new set, from either shop, gets a story — what inspired it, what's inside, and a few ideas for putting it to work in your own projects.

A little tease. Every so often there's something free — a sample design or a printable treat — but those live with my email list, not here on the blog. Join the list and the freebies come straight to your inbox; first up when you join is my 60-page list of things to sublimate. There's a signup box on the homepage.

Ideas. The art is only half the fun. The other half is what you make with it, so expect project ideas, round-ups, and seasonal inspiration along the way.

Everything in both shops is an instant download, so when a post makes you want to start making, you can start the same day. Thanks for stopping by — the kettle's on and the pencils are out.
