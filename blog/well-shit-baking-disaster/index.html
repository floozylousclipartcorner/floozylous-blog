Well Shit… Christmas Baking, Honestly

Let's be honest about Christmas baking. Sometimes the cookies are perfect. Sometimes the kitchen looks like a crime scene and you're eating the evidence.

This one is for the baker who laughs instead of cries. For the friend whose gingerbread house fell down — twice. For the maker who knows the funny ones sell first at a craft fair.

Press it for your own Christmas kitchen. Gift it to the baker in your life. Or make a stack for craft fair season — the honest ones are always the first to go.

Burnt cookies? Wonky frosting? It's fine. It's Christmas. Well shit… pass the spatula.
