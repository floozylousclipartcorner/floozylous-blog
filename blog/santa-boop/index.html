The Boop Heard Round the Barnyard

Some Christmas designs are pretty. This one is funny — and funny is what stops people at a craft fair table.

This one is for the cow lovers. For the person on your list who would rather laugh than be fancy. For the maker who wants one piece in the booth that does the talking all by itself.

Press it for your own Christmas kitchen. Make a few for the cocoa bar. Gift it to the hostess who has everything. Or run off a whole stack for craft fair season — this is the one people pick up, giggle at, and carry to the till.

Santa clearly has a favourite cow this year. Boop.
