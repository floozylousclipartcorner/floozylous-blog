Christmas Gifts to Press (That They'll Actually Use)

Some gifts get smiled at, thanked for, and quietly re-gifted by New Year's. These are not those gifts.

Every one of these is something you press yourself — a cutting board that lives on the counter, coasters that catch every mug — the kind of gift that gets used every single day instead of stored in a closet. Here's what's on my gift list this year.

For the friend who laughs at everything: the Melting Snowman set. A cutting board and four matching coasters with sarcastic holiday sayings in icy blue glitter — the funniest thing in the kitchen, and it gets funnier every time someone reads it.

For the hostess with the menorah: the Hanukkah board. A glowing golden menorah with challah and candlelit details, made for the table that hosts all eight nights.

For the person of faith on your list: the With God board. "With God All Things Are Possible" in warm autumn colours, made for the kitchen where the coffee's always on.

For the stockings: coasters. Quick to press, easy to gift, and everybody needs one under their mug. The coaster section has the whole collection.

And if you're shopping for yourself too — watch for discounts, they pop up all through the season.
